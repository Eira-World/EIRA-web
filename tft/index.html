EIRA · TFT

Everything you need between two rounds.

The Teamfight Tactics side of EIRA: what a unit brings, who else has that trait, what two components build into — and what actually places well, counted from real ranked games.

In development

What it answers

Units

Every unit of the current set, grouped by cost, with its traits and ability. The order TFT players actually think in.

Traits

Who else carries this trait, and what happens at two, four, six. The question you have with a unit already in your hand.

Items

A grid of the nine components in row and column, with the result in every cell — the whole combination table at once, instead of reading recipes one by one.

Augments

What the three things you were just offered actually do, while the timer runs.

The numbers

Placements, not win rates. In TFT, finishing second is not a loss — average placement is what the game is actually scored on.

Average placement per unit, trait, item and augment, counted from ranked matches EIRA samples itself: TFT-LEAGUE-V1 → PUUID → TFT-MATCH-V1. Nothing is scraped from other statistics sites. Every figure carries its sample size, and one with too small a sample is shown plainly instead of being dressed up as a finding.

Set data — units, traits, items, augments and icons — comes from Data Dragon and Community Dragon.

What it does not do

Riot provides no live interface for Teamfight Tactics. The board, the items on it, gold and round number are not available to any third-party tool, and EIRA does not pretend otherwise — the view states plainly what it cannot see.

  • No gameplay automation of any kind.
  • No reading or modifying game memory.
  • No screen capture of the board.
  • The API key is stored encrypted on the machine, never logged.
  • Free. No ads, no paid features.
