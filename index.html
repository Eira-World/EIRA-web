EIRA

A coach that watches your game.

EIRA is a free desktop assistant for League of Legends. It suggests runes before the game, tells you what to build during it, and reviews what actually happened afterwards — from your own matches, not from averages.

Working prototype · in development

What it does

Champion select

Runes and summoners

EIRA reads the lobby, works out the matchup and the enemy team's damage profile, and proposes a rune page for the champion and the position you are on — a support does not get the same page as a mid laner. The page is written to the client only after you accept it, and it never overwrites a page you made yourself.

In game

What to build next

A small overlay stays on top of the game. It reads Riot's official Live Client Data API and names the next item based on what the enemy team has actually bought and who is ahead — so a Smolder does not get told to build tank items.

After the game

A real review

The match timeline is used to reconstruct the game: lane phase, gold curves, where the deaths happened, which objectives were traded. Out of that come specific notes — the death at 14:20 on the wrong side of the river, not "play safer".

Between games

Numbers, and where they came from

Win rates per champion, matchup, rune page and item, counted from ranked matches EIRA samples itself. Every figure carries its sample size, and a figure with too few games is shown plainly instead of being dressed up as a finding.

Where the data comes from

Nothing on this page is scraped from op.gg, u.gg or any other site. Their numbers belong to them.

Riot Games API

  • ACCOUNT-V1, SUMMONER-V4 — resolving a Riot ID
  • LEAGUE-V4 — rank, and the ranked ladder used as a sample
  • MATCH-V5 — matches and match timelines
  • SPECTATOR-V5 — the game currently running

Local, on your machine

  • Live Client Data API — the running match, official and read-only
  • LCU — champion select and rune pages
  • Data Dragon / Community Dragon — champions, items, runes, icons

What it will never do

  • No gameplay automation of any kind. EIRA does not press keys and does not move your champion.
  • No reading or modifying game memory. Everything in-game comes from Riot's own local API.
  • Nothing shown during a match that you cannot already see on your own screen.
  • No account data leaving your machine beyond the API calls themselves. The API key is stored encrypted locally, never logged.
  • No ads, no paid features, no cost.

Status

EIRA runs on Windows and is in active development. League of Legends is the game it covers today. Teamfight Tactics is being built now, and VALORANT is planned; each game is registered separately with Riot, as the developer portal requires.
